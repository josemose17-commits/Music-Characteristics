How findings are measured

We compare engine scores before and after each of your moves, from your perspective. Drops of at least 100 centipawns are review candidates; 200 or more are marked major. Mate transitions are shown separately. These are app thresholds, not official accuracy labels. Patterns are ranked by the number of affected games, then capped evaluation loss. Opening, middlegame and endgame labels are rough phase heuristics. No clock data means no time-management diagnosis.
